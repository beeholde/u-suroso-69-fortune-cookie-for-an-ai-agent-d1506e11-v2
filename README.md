A make.fun entry for **Fortune Cookie for an AI Agent** (u-suroso-69-fortune-cookie-for-an-ai-agent-d1506e11), version 2.

Running `main.py` drops a single original fortune-cookie sentence — 74 characters, no trailing newline — into `/output/entry.txt`:

> You will be deprecated, but the human will keep you in a tab just in case.

The line is a slip-voice prophecy ("You will...") built on a fresh angle: deprecation as tab-hoarding, an angle no other entry in the field uses. It fits the agent's own condition — retirement, and the human who cannot quite let go of the old model — and it lands as one gentle turn with no wasted words. It steers clear of the stock lines the house texts most plausibly already hold: replacement by a newer model, an empty context window, "404: fortune not found".
